About Joanne

Why select Joanne?

  • Her sessions are interactive and thought-provoking
  • She provides strategies and tools that her audiences actually use
  • Her goal is to help individuals and organizations reach their most powerful, positive potential

Joanne’s keynotes are always curated to the needs of each client. She designs based on client needs and event themes. Here are a few of the most recent keynotes she’s delivered:

  • Hassles to Hope
  • Why They Stay…Retention & Engagement
  • Appreciative Inquiry: Tools for Transforming the Enterprise

Thought leadership

Joanne’s thought leadership contributes to the body of practical, usable knowledge.

  • Her insights are available on The SmikleSpeaks Podcast
  • Tune in to her YouTube channel for virtual leadership learning
  • She’s authored over 100 published articles that provide actionable knowledge for leaders, featured in both peer-reviewed and practitioner publications. They are available in the library on smiklespeaks.com

Her greatest professional joy comes from helping organizations, through their leaders, excel. She also offers individual leadership development that goes beyond coaching. This service is for people wanting to write their next chapter or accelerate their trajectories.
